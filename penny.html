Penny's Food & Treats

Morning
  • Feed her 1 ziploc bag of chicken and rice, and open her probiotic capsule and spread it on top of the food.

Afternoon
  • Feed her 1 ziploc bag of chicken and rice.
  • Wash both bowls and refill her water bowl.
Treats
  • You can give her up to half a cup of chicken once a day. Put pieces in her toys, or use it to get her to listen to you.

Scout's Food

Morning
  • Half a can of cat food — put the other half in a glass container in the fridge. She's usually in the front of the house in the morning.
  • Wash her water bowl and refill it.
Afternoon
  • Put out the other half of the cat food from the glass container. She's sometimes in the back of the house in the afternoon.
  • If she's not there either in the morning or afternoon, just leave the food outside — she'll eat it eventually.
